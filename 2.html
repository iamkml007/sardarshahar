Chronicles of Bikaner State

Architectural Legacy of Sardarshahar

Established in 1838 by Maharaja Sardar Singh before his ascension to the throne of Bikaner, Sardarshahar was laid out with wide geometric avenues radiating around the central bazaar and Ghanta Ghar.

The town is celebrated for its grand merchant Havelis featuring Shekhawati frescoes, carved Dulmera red sandstone facades, domed chhatris, and intricate wooden craftsmanship.

Painted Havelis

19th-century merchant mansions with natural mineral murals.

Handcrafted Woodwork

Renowned across Rajasthan for intricate carved doors & furniture.

Historical Milestones

1838 AD

Foundation by Maharaja Sardar Singh

Establishment of the fortified town and central market layout in northern Bikaner territory.

Late 19th Century

Golden Era of Grand Havelis & Ghanta Ghar

Construction of multi-courtyard sandstone havelis, stepwells (kunds), and the Clock Tower.

1916 – 1950

Railway & Educational Expansion

Connected via railway; founding of Gandhi Vidya Mandir (GVM) as a premier educational hub.

Modern Era

Growing Commercial & Civic Center

A thriving municipal hub of Churu district blending heritage tourism, agriculture, and enterprise.
